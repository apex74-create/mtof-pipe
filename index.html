MTOF Pipe — the house transport, standing

This subdomain is the transit host of the house transport (MTOF — Multiplex TOken Format) of Apex Air Solutions. What began as a one-shot proof on November 21, 2026 now stays transport: a standing cycle runs every 30 minutes — a fresh heartbeat payload is converted to tokens at the origin, its envelope is published here, an independent far-end receiver fetches it over plain HTTP (no TLS in the path; the token method is the layer), un-converts it, and verifies the hash.

Every cycle grades itself PASS, INTERRUPTED, or TAMPERED and appends the result to the pipe's own public diary:

  • Transport log (readable)
  • Transport log (JSON)
  • Current envelope — the resting token envelope of the latest cycle

If you leave a pipe open, someone is going to come and check it. This log is how the pipe says what it saw.
