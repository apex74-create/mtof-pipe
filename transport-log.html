MTOF Standing Transport — the pipe's diary

This subdomain is the transit host of the house transport (MTOF — Multiplex TOken Format, Apex Air Solutions). A standing cycle runs every 30 minutes: a fresh heartbeat payload is tokenized at the origin, published here as envelope.mtof.json, fetched back over plain HTTP by an independent receiver, un-converted, and hash-verified. Every cycle's grade is appended to this log and never rewritten.

Grades: PASS — bytes arrived exactly as sent and the payload reassembled hash-identical. INTERRUPTED — a leg of the transport failed (the failed leg is named). TAMPERED — bytes changed in transit or at rest.
